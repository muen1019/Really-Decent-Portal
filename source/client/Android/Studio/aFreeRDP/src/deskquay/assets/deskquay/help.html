Using the remote desktop

Use the Touch / Touchpad button on the session toolbar to change how your fingers interact with the remote screen.

  • Touch. Tap and swipe directly on the remote Windows desktop when it accepts remote touch.
  • Touchpad. Slide one finger to move the pointer. Tap with one finger to click, tap with two fingers to right-click, or slide two fingers to scroll.

Double-tap and drag

In Touchpad mode, tap twice and slide your finger while holding the second tap to drag an item. Release your finger to drop it.

To change this gesture, open Settings → User Interface → Double-tap and drag. It starts enabled. When disabled, a quick double tap still double-clicks, and sliding on the second tap moves the pointer.

The switch applies to the screen touchpad and compatible gestures handled by the app. For a Book Cover or another physical touchpad, Android controls native tap dragging in the device’s touchpad settings where available. You can still hold the physical primary button and move to drag.
