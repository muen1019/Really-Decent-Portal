## 0.1.10

- On the screen trackpad, slow swipes move the pointer more precisely. A quick swipe carries the pointer briefly in the same direction after you lift your finger and gradually slows to a stop. Touch the screen again to stop it immediately.
- When the desktop is zoomed in or the on-screen keyboard covers part of it, the view follows the screen-trackpad pointer near the edge of the visible area.
- Added more padding around the artwork in the launcher and README icons.

## 0.1.8

### Screen touchpad

- In screen touchpad mode, double-tap with one finger and move while the second touch is still down to drag with the left button. The second touch can land slightly away from the first. Two quick taps released without dragging still double-click.
- For a right-click, tap with two fingers or hold one finger still and release it. Double-tap with two fingers and move while the second touch is still down to drag with the right button.
- Swipe with two fingers to scroll the remote content vertically or horizontally. Pinch or spread to resize the entire remote desktop view on the Android screen.

With **Double-tap and drag** enabled, a single tap can wait for Android’s double-tap timeout before sending the click. Turn off the setting for clicks on lift.
